Machine Setup

Pico

  1. Add 2 inches of water into the step filter
  2. Insert the step filter with lid into Pico
  3. Attach the IN and OUT hoses to PicoStill
  4. Check that water reservoir is full

Zymatic

For an illustrated manual, see link
You will need:
    Keg Adapter Set
    5 Gallon Corny Keg
    Zymatic Transfer Tube Assembly
Setting up:
  1. Add 2.5 gallons of tap water to the 5 gallon corny keg
  2. Seal the keg with the Keg Seal, leaving the hole open (no foam trap or air lock) and slip the neoprene Keg Cozy over the keg to help prevent heat loss
  3. Connect the hose with the grey ball lock fitting to the 'IN' post of the keg
  4. Connect the fitting on one end of the clear Zymatic Transfer Tube assembly to the 'OUT' post of the keg
  5. Assemble the keg adapters, making sure that the silicone pieces are inserted completely into the black plastic pieces, creating a tight and secure fitting
  6. Attach the black fitting on the other end of your Zymatic Transfer Tube assembly to the Grey silicone keg fitting adapter, and connect this to the port on the still
  7. Attach the Black 'Racking' silicone fitting adapter to the black ball lock fitting at the end of the other Zymatic hose. Remove the small black plug from the port on the still, and connect the adapter to the still
